Flange Contact Area

Pick a joint and see where the ID and OD of the two mating faces meet. The faces only touch where both exist, so the contact ring runs from the larger ID to the smaller OD. Everything is drawn to scale (the pipe stubs are cut short).

Rough sketch tool. Flange sizes are approximate ASME B16.5 Class 150 values and the HDPE adapter and stub end dimensions are placeholders that vary by manufacturer, so overwrite them with your drawing or vendor data. Raised-face height, bolt holes and fillet radii are ignored: this compares faces only (add a gasket in the Joint panel if you want one between them).
