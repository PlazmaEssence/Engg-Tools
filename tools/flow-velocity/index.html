Pipe Flow & Velocity

Pick a pipe, size and rating to autofill OD, wall thickness and inner diameter, then enter a flow rate to get the velocity — or a velocity to get the required flow. Everything runs in your browser.

Engineering aid only. Carbon-steel dimensions follow ASME B36.10M and HDPE uses IPS ODs with wall = OD ÷ DR — always verify size, schedule/DR and wall against the current standard and your project spec. OD, wall and ID stay editable; edit ID and the wall recomputes, edit wall and the ID recomputes.
