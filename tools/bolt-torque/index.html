Bolt Torque

Tightening torque for a bolted joint using the short-form torque equation. Fill in either the total clamping load or the torque per bolt and the other one is calculated for you. Optionally enter the clamped ID and OD to also get the clamping stress.

The nut factor K lumps thread and under-head friction together and varies a lot with lubrication and finish (K = 0.16 is a typical value for plain, lightly oiled steel). Scatter of ±25% in preload is normal for torque tightening, so treat results as an estimate. The clamping load is the total for the joint, shared equally by the bolts.
