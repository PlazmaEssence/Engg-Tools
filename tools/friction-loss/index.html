Calculation steps

Method

Head loss h = (f·L/D + K) · v²/2g

Haaland: 1/√f = -1.8·log10[ (ε/D / 3.7)^1.11 + 6.9/Re ], valid for Re above 4000 and ε/D up to 0.05. Laminar flow (Re below 2300) uses f = 64/Re.

Pressure ΔP = ρ·g·h with g = 9.80665 m/s². Per-length results divide the total over the check distance. Pipe dimensions are an aid only: CS per ASME B36.10M, HDPE on IPS OD with wall = OD / DR. Verify against the current standard and your project spec.
